# Portable layout blueprints

Normalized coordinates use x=0 at left, y=0 at top, width/height=100. Ranges describe the bundled masters closely enough to execute without seeing them. Preserve the relationships, not pixel-perfect boxes. A hero may cross a listed boundary only where the blueprint says so.

## R1 — long diagonal architecture

- Best for: long wall, horizontal facade, corridor, processional line.
- Display title: x 1–90, y 0–32; two stacked lines, left aligned, each about 15–17 high; nearly touches top/left. Narrow metadata strip x 91–99.
- Local rail: x 2–15, y 34–74; large vertical Chinese title, then small location/body copy. Keep it visibly separate from the wall.
- Hero: long photographic architecture enters from right and rises diagonally from about x 18/y 75 to x 100/y 18; occupies roughly half the canvas and is the first fixation.
- Support: upper-middle ink field behind hero; lower-left sculptural fragment; lower-right dark torn mass. These overlap hero locally rather than forming cards.
- Paper channels: irregular cream seams around the diagonal and along the left rail, not equal gutters.
- Z-order: paper/ink fields -> processed fragments -> giant title -> hero -> one small foreground fragment.
- Allowed crossing: hero may cover lower portions of the second English line; lower foreground fragment may cover a small hero corner.

## R2 — giant edge subject

- Best for: statue face, relief, mask, round carving or close-up with a strong silhouette.
- Display title: x 1–88, y 0–32; two enormous left-aligned lines. First line may approach the right edge behind the hero.
- Local rail: x 3–16, y 34–74; large vertical Chinese title with compact metadata beneath.
- Hero: realistic close-up enters from right/top-right, x 61–100 and y 8–100; face/object may bleed off top, right and bottom; unmistakable first fixation.
- Structural context: slanted building/plaque mass x 16–76, y 34–88 behind hero.
- Support: monochrome lower-left fragment and one broken color/rubbing field behind the context. Avoid a separate right-side card strip.
- Paper channels: left rail plus torn seams between context and hero.
- Z-order: paper/print fields -> processed context -> title/rail -> hero -> tiny seal.
- Allowed crossing: hero covers a moderate right portion of title/context; context may cross behind the rail only at one narrow point.

## R3 — axial gate or tower

- Best for: central gate, nested arches, tower, pagoda or upward axial architecture.
- Left rail: x 0–14, y 0–100; series metadata at top, large vertical Chinese title from about y 27–55, location/body below. Keep this full-height paper column.
- Display title: x 14–62, y 0–35; two stacked giant lines, left aligned. A small portion may disappear behind the hero/evidence.
- Hero: central photographic architecture x 20–92, y 28–88; rise toward upper middle. For gates, make the dark tunnel/arch void a strong value mass and let the crown enter the second title line.
- Upper-right evidence: x 58–100, y 0–36; monochrome/halftone detail partly behind the hero.
- Lower collage: two or three diagonal/stepped fragments across x 0–90, y 69–100, with partial overlaps and unequal tears.
- Quiet paper: left rail and one slim irregular seam, not broad empty lower corners.
- Z-order: paper/ink ground -> upper evidence -> title/rail -> lower processed fragments -> hero -> one small foreground fragment/seal.
- Allowed crossing: hero crown overlaps up to roughly one fifth of second title line; lower fragment overlaps a small hero base; no support layer covers the tunnel's central depth.

## R4 — tall center-right figure

- Best for: tall statue, seated figure, stele, vessel or vertical artifact.
- Left rail: x 0–14, y 0–100; series metadata, large vertical Chinese title, location/body copy.
- Display title: x 14–88, y 0–31; two stacked lines; first line very large, second somewhat smaller.
- Hero: tall realistic figure x 48–88, y 15–96; monumental and slightly cropped, first fixation.
- Structural context: diagonal architecture x 12–65, y 34–82 behind hero.
- Far-right strip: x 88–100, y 4–100; 2–3 cropped processed details with irregular internal joins, not equal tiles.
- Lower support: dark relief/stone/woodcut fragments x 14–63, y 73–100, partly under hero.
- Z-order: paper/color fields -> right strip/context -> title/rail -> lower fragments -> hero -> tiny seal.
- Allowed crossing: hero may cover a small right end of title and much of context; strip remains visibly secondary.

## Universal thumbnail checks

At 10% size, the eye should land on the real hero first and the giant title second. One dark anchor, one warm/colored print field and one neutral paper channel should create a clear three-value rhythm. If the poster reads as multiple equal cards, scattered stickers, a generic centered photo, or a title sitting safely above all imagery, the selected blueprint was not followed.
